# Delivery and Postnatal Workflow

1. A midwife records an outcome for an active pregnancy.
2. Future Manila dates and duplicate outcomes are rejected.
3. A midwife or assigned nurse records same-resident postnatal follow-up.
4. Completed status requires delivered status and postnatal follow-up.
5. Administrators can archive metadata through a trusted RPC.

Narratives never enter appointment list, queue, or calendar APIs.
