# Child Growth Workflow

1. A midwife creates a child profile from an active registry resident.
2. Registry birth date must match; parent links grant no clinical access.
3. Midwives and assigned nurses record growth. BHW entry additionally requires
   a checked-in or in-progress Child Health appointment.
4. At least one plausibly bounded measurement is required.
5. Age and BMI are never stored or automatically classified.
