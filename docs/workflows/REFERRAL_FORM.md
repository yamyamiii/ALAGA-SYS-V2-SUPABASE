# Referral Form

> Preserved as an inactive future extension and excluded from the approved
> final thesis scope. No Referral Form action is visible in the application.

Referral creation is a controlled clinical workflow linked to one signed or
amended encounter. Only that encounter's attending nurse or an attending
midwife on a maternal/child encounter may author it.

1. Open the signed encounter and choose **Create Referral Form**.
2. Enter the receiving facility, clinician-authored reason, and concise relevant
   clinical summary.
3. Save the draft. The request key makes a repeated create idempotent.
4. Review and explicitly finalize. Finalized content is immutable.
5. Preview, print, or download the finalized referral.

The resident is derived from the encounter. The browser never submits a
resident ID. A resident may view only their own finalized referral through an
encounter they can already view. Administrators and BHWs receive no referral
narrative access.

Archiving is limited to the referring clinician and removes the document from
routine viewing. Audits record semantic lifecycle metadata only. Referral
reason and summary are never generated by AI, inferred from diagnosis, logged,
or sent to third parties.
